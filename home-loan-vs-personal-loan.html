Home loan vs personal loan

Last updated: 25 September 2026

A home loan and a personal loan both give you a lump sum that you repay in monthly instalments, but they are built differently. The right one depends on what you are borrowing for and what you can offer as security.

Main differences

  • Security. A home loan is secured against the property you buy. A personal loan is usually unsecured, so the lender relies on your income and credit history.
  • Interest rates. Because a home loan is secured, its rate is usually lower than a personal loan's rate for the same borrower.
  • Tenure. Home loans can run for up to 30 years in India. Personal loans are usually much shorter, often a few years.
  • Fees. Both can carry a processing fee, plus GST on that fee. Compare the total cost, not just the rate.
  • Tax treatment. Interest on a home loan may qualify for deductions under the Income Tax Act, subject to conditions and the tax regime you choose. Interest on a personal loan used for personal expenses generally does not. Check with a tax professional.

Compare with the same tools

Enter both offers into the Loan Comparison Calculator. It shows each EMI, total interest, processing fee with GST, total cost and the effective annual rate, which is the true cost once fees are included.

A shorter tenure on a personal loan can produce a higher EMI with less total interest, even at a higher rate. The EMI Calculator lets you test different tenures on the same amount.

Questions to ask the lender

  • What is the annual rate, and is it fixed or floating?
  • What is the processing fee, and what is the GST on it?
  • Are there prepayment or foreclosure charges?
  • What is the total amount payable over the full tenure?
