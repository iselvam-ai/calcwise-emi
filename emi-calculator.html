Under the Hood

How EMI is Calculated in India

Indian banking institutions calculate standard reducing balance EMIs via standard mathematical annuity formulation:

Equated Monthly Installment Formula
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n - 1)
P

Principal Sum

The borrowed credit disbursed.

r

Monthly Rate

Annual rate ÷ (12 × 100).

n

Duration

Total count of monthly tenures.

Loan Advisory & Principles

When you prepay towards the loan principal, your outstanding principal decreases immediately. Because interest is charged on the reducing balance, subsequent interest installments drop significantly, shortening your loan tenure or reducing subsequent EMIs.
Floating interest rates move with RBI’s repo rate fluctuations. If repo rates soften, your effective interest decreases. Fixed rates lock in the same interest rate throughout your chosen duration, protecting against macroeconomic rate hikes but typically commanding a 1-2% initial premium.
Indian scheduled banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) segregate borrower brackets by credit scores. Scores of 750+ typically unlock the lowest benchmark card rates (currently ~8.50%), while scores below 700 may incur risk-premiums of 50–150 basis points.
Yes, under the Old Tax Regime in India: Principal repayment is eligible for deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Section 80C. Interest payment is eligible for deduction up to ₹2.0 Lakhs under Section 24(b) for self-occupied properties.
