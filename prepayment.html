Institutional Debt Strategies & Pro-Tips

01

Front-Loaded Savings

Prepaying early in your tenure delivers maximum compound interest savings. In the first 5 years of an Indian home loan, up to 70% of each regular EMI covers pure interest.

bolt Impact: 3x greater in Year 1 vs Year 10
02

The 1-Extra EMI Strategy

Paying just one additional EMI each calendar year via annual performance bonus cuts an ordinary 20-year home loan by over 4 years without tightening monthly cash-flow.

calendar_month Simple, habit-based debt reduction
03

Prepay vs SIP Arbitrage

Prepaying yields a guaranteed tax-free return equal to your lending rate (8.50%). Compare this against mutual fund equity SIPs, accounting for market volatility and 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax.
